## Printer presets: large format and production
Add these to the Printer preset (and their resolutions to the DPI list where missing). Check each against the
maker's specs before adding; lab machines vary by model and setup.

- Canon imagePROGRAF PRO-1000 and larger (PRO-2000/4000/6000): 600
- Epson SureColor P5000 and larger: 720 (1440 fine)
- HP DesignJet Z-series: 600
- Roland / Mimaki large-format inkjet: 720 (1440 fine)
- DNP DS620 / DS820, Mitsubishi CP-D70, Kodak 6900 (dye-sub): 300
- Fuji Frontier: 300 (some models 320)
- Noritsu QSS: 320 (some newer models 400)
- Durst Lambda: 200 or 400
- Océ LightJet: 200 or 300
- HP Indigo: 812
- Xerox Iridesse / production laser: 2400
- Offset (imagesetter / platesetter): 2400 or 2540
