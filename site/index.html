Lenticular printing

Prints that move when you tilt them.

Lenticulator turns your photos into interlaced images for lenticular lens sheets: 3D depth or flip animation, matched to your lens and your printer. It runs in your browser or as a desktop app, and your images never leave your computer.

Features

From photos to a print you can line up under the lens

Automatic alignment

Frames shot by hand or with a sliding camera rarely line up. Lenticulator corrects shift, rotation and zoom to a fraction of a pixel, or lines every frame up on one subject so a 3D print keeps its depth.

  • Onion skin, difference, flicker and red/cyan overlays
  • Drag or nudge any frame by hand

3D from a single photo

No stereo camera? Estimate depth from one photo on your own computer, choose how deep the effect should be and which distance sits at the print surface, and generate as many views as your lens can show.

  • Or load your own depth map, for example from a phone's portrait mode
  • Frame count follows your printer and lens (DPI ÷ LPI)

See it before you print

Move the mouse across the preview to tilt the print. Check depth with red/cyan glasses, play animations back, and export the preview as an animated GIF or video to share.

Exact interlacing for your lens

Any lens pitch, including fractional LPI, and any printer resolution. Strips are anti-aliased so they stay accurate when the dots per lens aren't a whole number, and every export carries the print DPI so it comes out at the right size.

  • PNG, JPG and TIFF export, or print directly at actual size
  • Lens alignment bars and crop marks in the margin

Calibrate with test patterns

A lens rarely matches the pitch printed on its packaging at your viewing distance. Print the pitch test, find the band that flips cleanly between black and white, and use that LPI. A lens ruler and a frame-order test are included too.

Flip and animation

Switch to Animation for motion and flip effects. Horizontal lenses let both eyes see the same frame, so animations stay clean. Try it with Eadweard Muybridge's The Horse in Motion from the built-in samples.

Every common format

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF (multi-page), Photoshop PSD (layers as frames) and MPO files from 3D cameras. Drop in whole folders.

Private and offline

All processing, including depth estimation, happens on your computer. The desktop app works without an internet connection.

Fast

Rendering uses every CPU core, so even large prints at high printer resolution are ready in seconds.
