Features

Turn your photos into lens-ready prints.

Automagical Alignment 

Hand-held shots and sliding-camera frames never line up on their own. Lenticulator fixes shift, rotation and zoom to a fraction of a pixel, or locks every frame onto your subject so your 3D prints keep their depth.

  • Check your work with onion skin, difference, flicker and red/cyan overlays
  • Nudge any frame by hand when you want the final say

3D from a single photo

No stereo camera? No problem. Turn one photo into a full set of 3D views, right from the comfort of your own computer. Choose how deep the effect looks, pick the focus point of the print or wigglegram, and generate as many "camera views" as you'd like.

  • Already have a depth map, like one from your iPhone's portrait mode? Bring it along.
  • The number of views is auto matched to your printer and lens sheet specs.

Know it works before you print

Tilt the preview with your mouse and watch the print come alive. Check depth with red/cyan glasses, play animations back, and share the result as an animated GIF or video, all before you spend a sheet.

Any lens. Any printer.

Any lens pitch, even fractional LPI, and any printer resolution. Anti-aliased strips stay accurate when the dots per lens aren't a whole number, and every export carries the print DPI, so it prints at exactly the size you set.

  • Export PNG, JPG or TIFF, or print straight at actual size
  • Alignment bars and crop marks in the margin make lining up the lens easy

Dial in your lens sheets

A lens rarely matches the pitch printed on its packaging. Print the pitch test, find the band that flips cleanly between black and white, and you've found your true LPI. A lens ruler and a frame-order test are included too, so every print after that comes out right.

Make it move

Switch to Animation for flip and motion effects. Tilt the print and it plays through your frames like a flipbook, from a spinning planet to a before-and-after. Try the spinning Earth or Muybridge's animals in motion from the built-in samples.

Bring any photo

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, multi-page TIFF, Photoshop PSD (layers as frames) and MPO files from 3D cameras. Drop in whole folders and go.

Pick up where you left off

Save your frames, alignment and every print setting in one project file. Reopen it any time and carry on.

Built for speed

Rendering uses your graphics card, or every CPU core, so even big prints at high printer resolution are ready in seconds.

Wigglegrams

Wiggle it, just a little bit.

Go on, post it to your Instagram or whatever

A wigglegram rocks back and forth between views shot a little apart. Your brain reads the shift between near and far as depth. No glasses, no lens, and it plays anywhere a GIF does.

  • Load two or more views of a scene, or create them from a single photo with Depth
  • Pick a key point on your subject and every frame locks onto it
  • Export a GIF and set the speed that feels just right
